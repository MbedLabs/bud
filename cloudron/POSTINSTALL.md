Open Bud at $CLOUDRON-APP-ORIGIN and create your administrator account.

The instance starts with no accounts, so the first visit shows a setup screen
that asks for your name, email address and a password. It stops being reachable
the moment the account exists — nobody else can claim the instance.

Email — invitations, password resets and address changes — works only if you
enabled the Email addon for this app. Without it, the administrator you create
here is the only way in.
